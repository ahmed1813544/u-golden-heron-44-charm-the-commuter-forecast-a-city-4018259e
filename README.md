One line of weather forecast (140 characters at most) for a city that does not exist — written to make a bored commuter smile on the way to work.

## The entry

`main.py` (stdlib only) writes a single forecast line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`):

> Good morning, Puddlewick: light rain, 13 degrees, and the ducks have taken the bus lane again — allow extra time, and wave back.

Puddlewick is invented. The forecast keeps the rhythm of broadcast weather — greeting, place, conditions, temperature — then bends it with one concrete local detail (the ducks in the bus lane) and a small smile at the end.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
